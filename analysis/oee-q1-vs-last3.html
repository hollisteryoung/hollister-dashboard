Key insights

The company is flat, not recovering

Company OEE moved 54.7% → 54.1% — inside the noise band. Five families improved and five declined, so the gains and losses cancelled. Nothing structural changed between the two quarters.

Biggest deterioration: 1-Piece Autocoiners

Down 7.0 pts (64.5% → 57.5%) — the largest drop of any multi-machine family, and it is concentrated: Autocoiner #5 fell 75.1% → 43.8% and Autocoiner #3 fell 67.7% → 40.6%. The other ten machines held. Two assets erased the family's lead.

Biggest improvement: Urostomy cells

Up 7.6 pts (39.2% → 46.8%), driven almost entirely by HU2 recovering 18.4% → 38.1%. Still 7.9 pts below target, but this is the clearest sustained gain in the fleet.

Only family beating target

Dansac Drainable Pouch at 60.8% vs. a 56.4% target (+4.4 pts), up 4.9 pts on Q1 as P16 climbed 42.3% → 61.6%. Every other family is below target.

Scale still points at BIM

BIM is 17 of 64 machines (27% of the fleet) and improved slightly (+1.0 pt) — but at 54.7% it remains 16.4 pts below target. Its spread is the story: BIM 7 runs 78%, while BIM 2 sits at 20% and BIM 1 at 24%.

Dead assets are distorting the averages

Extruder 4 has logged 0.0% every month of 2026 and Kiefel 2 averages 11.8%; HF12 sits at 14.4% with no target set. These three drag Ring/Extruder to 37.0% and Dansac Closed to 40.5%. Confirm status before treating either family gap as a performance problem.

Recommended focus

  1. Reconcile the zero-output assets first. Extruder 4, Kiefel 2 and HF12 need an idle/decommissioned ruling before any family average is trusted.
  2. Root-cause Autocoiner #3 and #5. A 28–31 pt fall in one quarter on two machines is the single largest recoverable loss on the board.
  3. Run BIM as a programme, not a machine list. With 17 assets and a 16 pt gap, closing the BIM spread moves company OEE more than any other family.
